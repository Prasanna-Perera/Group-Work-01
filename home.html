Anuththarian Victories

Anuththara College proudly celebrates a monumental achievement in its sporting history as Sudam Liyanage has been appointed Captain of the Sri Lanka Under-18 National Basketball Team, bringing immense honour and pride to the College community. This prestigious appointment is a testament to Sudam’s outstanding talent, unwavering dedication, exemplary leadership, and years of hard work on and off the court. His achievement reflects the true spirit of Anuththara college and the strong sporting tradition that continues to produce athletes of national calibre. As he takes on the responsibility of leading the national team, the entire Anuththara family stands proudly behind him and wishes him every success in guiding Sri Lanka to victory on the international stage. Adding to this proud occasion, Anuththara College also congratulates Amavin on his selection as a reserve player for the Sri Lanka Under-18 National Basketball Team. Earning recognition at national level is a remarkable accomplishment, and Amavin’s selection highlights his commitment, discipline, and exceptional potential in the sport.
