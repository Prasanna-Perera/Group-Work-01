Clubs & Societies Chronicle

The Duke of Edinburgh’s International Award is a globally recognized youth development programme designed for young people aged 14 to 24. Established in 1956 by His Royal Highness Prince Philip and educationalist Sir Kurt Hahn, the programme operates in over 130 countries, including Sri Lanka, and focuses on developing well-rounded individuals through four key pillars: Voluntary Service, Skills, Physical Recreation, and the Adventurous Journey. In Sri Lanka, the Award is administered by the National Youth Services Council (NYSC), and at Anuththara College, Colombo, the programme continues to thrive with approximately 40 students actively engaged in this prestigious international initiative.



Latest Highlights

A group of students representing the Student Parliament of Anuththara College, Colombo, visited the Presidential Secretariat and the President’s House this morning (24) as part of an educational visit. This opportunity was granted to the College in line with the “Vision” programme series, jointly organised by the Presidential Office, the Ministry of Education, and the Communication Division of the Parliament of Sri Lanka, aimed at educating school students on governance and civic responsibility. Following a tour of the historic Old Parliament building within the Presidential Secretariat premises, students were briefed on the history of Parliament and its evolution. They were also educated on the role of the Executive, the functions of the President’s Fund, and the importance of the rule of law. During the programme, certificates were awarded to members representing the Student Parliament of Anuththara College by the Deputy Speaker of Parliament, Hon. Risvi Salih. The event was attended by Presidential Legal Advisor Attorney-at-Law J. M. Wijebandara and Senior Additional Secretary to the President Subhash Roshan Gamage. Symbolising the importance of environmental conservation, a valuable sapling was also presented to the school. Officials including the Director and Deputy Directors of the Tri-Forces Security Coordination Unit, the Principal of Anuththara College Mr. Lal Dissanayake, and members of the academic staff were also present on this occasion.
